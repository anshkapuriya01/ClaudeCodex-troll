# Metaphor mapping: from a topic to a shot list

The difference between a "slideshow about X" and a produced video is that a produced video makes the viewer *feel* X through many unrelated images that share one quality. This file explains how to find that quality and fill a shot list with it.

## Step 1: Find the sensation word

Ask: "If this topic were a physical action, what would it be?" Choose a verb that a camera can film.

| Topic | Weak (abstract) | Strong (filmable) |
|---|---|---|
| Foldable phone screen cracking | "innovation", "problem" | **fold**, then **crack** |
| A faster AI model | "intelligence" | **speed**, **precision**, **click into place** |
| Stock market crash | "economy" | **fall**, **collapse** |
| A new privacy feature | "security" | **close**, **lock**, **shelter** |
| A sports comeback | "victory" | **rise**, **break through** |
| Climate report | "change" | **melt**, **dry out** |
| Company launch / new chapter | "future" | **open**, **ignite**, **lift off** |

Two words in sequence (fold → crack, build → break, close → open) give you a built-in story arc: the first word for the build, the second for the turn.

## Step 2: Fill every lane

For each lane, list 4–6 ideas. Write each as a searchable, literal description.

### Worked example: "fold" (then "crack") for a folding-phone durability story

| Lane | Shots |
|---|---|
| Literal | foldable phone opening on a table (no logo visible), hinge of a generic folding device in macro, phone screen with a hairline crack, slow-motion hand opening a phone |
| Mechanical | laptop lid closing, hydraulic press closing, door hinge swinging, folding chair snapping shut, garage door rolling down, piano lid closing |
| Human body | knee bending (runner, dancer), fingers folding into a fist, gymnast in a bridge, hand closing slowly, back curling in a stretch, eyelid closing in macro |
| Material | paper folded in half, origami crane being made, napkin folded at a restaurant, sheet of metal bent in a brake press, fabric folded on a table, map being folded, envelope sealed |
| Nature | leaf curling, mimosa plant closing when touched, flower bud closing in time-lapse, bird folding its wings, wave curling over |
| Architecture / scale | drawbridge rising, aerial of a folding stadium roof, origami-style architecture, accordion bus joint turning |
| Abstract / macro (for "crack") | ice cracking, dry mud cracks in time-lapse, glass shattering in slow motion, eggshell cracking, ceramic breaking, lightning |

Arc: hook on a hard cut of a crack sound over black → build with folds of everything, faster and faster, all moving the same direction → turn: one slow-motion fold, silence, a single crack sound → payoff: title "Some things weren't made to fold." → end card.

### Worked example: "speed / precision" for a new AI model launch

| Lane | Shots |
|---|---|
| Literal | a real screen recording of the product responding (make it yourself), keyboard close up, cursor moving across code |
| Mechanical | watch movement macro, gears meshing, a lock clicking shut, Formula 1 pit stop, a camera shutter, a typewriter key |
| Human body | sprinter's start, pianist's hands, a chef's knife cut, a calligrapher's stroke, an archer's release |
| Material | water drop in slow motion, ink in water, a crystal forming, pouring sand |
| Nature | hummingbird, falcon dive, lightning, a seed sprouting in time-lapse |
| Architecture / scale | city lights time-lapse, highway light trails, a train passing, aerial of a circuit-like city grid |
| Abstract | light leaks, focus pulls on glass, slow particles, a laser line |

## Step 3: Tag motion and shape

For every shot, write the motion direction and the dominant shape. Then group them:

- Same direction (all closing left→right) → montage runs and match-on-action cuts.
- Same shape (circle: an eye, a lens, a wheel, a coin, the sun) → graphic match cuts.
- Opposite direction → use only at the turn, where a reversal should feel jarring.

Plan 3+ match cuts per 30 s and write them as pairs: `origami fold (top→down)` → `laptop lid closing (top→down)` → `eyelid closing (top→down)`.

## Step 4: Check balance

- Literal shots ≤ about 1/3 of the edit.
- At least 4 lanes represented.
- At least one human body shot (people make it feel alive), one macro, and one wide/aerial (scale contrast).
- Nothing that requires explaining. Every metaphor should read in under a second.

## Step 5: Turn it into searches

For each shot, write 2–4 search queries, from specific to broad:

```
origami crane being made → "origami hands close up", "folding paper crane", "paper folding macro", "paper fold"
drawbridge rising       → "drawbridge opening", "bascule bridge", "bridge raising aerial"
mimosa closing          → "mimosa pudica touch", "sensitive plant closing", "leaves closing timelapse"
```

If the first query returns nothing good, try a synonym or a wider framing before dropping the shot.
